Format cheatsheet

Header

  • **Time=00:15:30**
  • **Title=...**
  • **Description=...**
  • **TotalMarks=100** (optional)
  • **ShuffleQuestions=false** (optional)

    • Questions

  • ## Question 1
  • **Type=multiple-choice-single**
  • **Type=multiple-choice-multi**
  • **Type=typed**
  • **Type=cloze**
  • **Marks=10**

    • Options

  • Single: - Sydney, correct: - *Canberra*
  • Multi: - [ ] wrong, - [*] correct
  • Cloze blanks: [[Paris]]

    • Typed model answer (optional)

  • **Answer=...** — a single-line model answer, hidden from the candidate and used when marking.
    • Copies a prompt containing these rules. Paste it into an AI assistant, fill in your exam details, and it returns a ready-to-use .md file.
